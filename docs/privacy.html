Doodle River Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Doodle River is a puzzle game made by Sarath Ramesh ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses on Android, iOS and the web, and why.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, location or any account. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect personal data ourselves.

Your level progress, stars and sound setting are saved only on your device and never leave it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads. On Android and iOS the ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model, app usage and ad interactions. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is shown, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time with "Privacy & ads" on the title screen.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised using your advertising identifier.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

On web game sites (such as Poki, CrazyGames or GameDistribution) ads are served by that site, and its own privacy policy applies.

Children

Doodle River is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
